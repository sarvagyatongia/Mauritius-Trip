Mauritius Driving Laws Cheat Sheet

  • Toddler Car Seat Law: Children under 10 years are strictly forbidden in the front seat. Your daughter must sit in her Group 1 car seat in the back row!
  • Left-Hand Traffic: Drives on the left side (same as India!). Clockwise roundabouts.
  • Speed Cameras: 100 km/h on M1 motorway, 40–50 km/h inside coastal villages. Automated radars strictly enforced.
